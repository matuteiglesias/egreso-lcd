# Content Authoring & Evidence Rules

## 1. Principle

The portal adds value by connecting fragmented information without erasing provenance.

Do not make all knowledge sound equally authoritative.

## 2. Claim categories

Use the registry types as editorial constraints.

### Official requirement

Example:
`El título secundario legalizado por UBA es requisito para iniciar el diploma.`

UI:
**Requisito oficial**

### Official process

Example:
`El seguimiento se realiza por TAD.`

UI:
**Proceso oficial**

### Official timing

A time published by an official source.

UI:
**Tiempo informado por la institución**

### Official recommendation

Advice explicitly contained in official/regulatory material.

UI:
**Recomendación**

### Community expectation

Operational experience used for planning.

UI:
**Tiempo orientativo** / **Referencia práctica**

Must state that it is not guaranteed.

## 3. Conservative timing

When practical timing information varies:

- communicate a conservative planning range/estimate;
- identify it as orientative;
- do not convert it into a promised deadline;
- do not fabricate statistical certainty.

Current editorial convention:

> **Diploma: calculá aproximadamente 8 meses como referencia práctica.**
> Puede variar; no es un plazo garantizado.

## 4. Freshness

Official does not automatically mean current.

Volatile information includes:

- dates;
- event schedules;
- jura slots;
- forms;
- payment instructions;
- workflow labels;
- operational contact details.

Before release, revalidate high-volatility claims.

Do not publish stale event dates simply because an official page still contains them.

## 5. Narrative page template

Each guidance page should answer:

1. **Qué es**
2. **Cuándo llegás a esta etapa**
3. **Antes de empezar**
4. **Qué tenés que hacer**
5. **Qué resultado deberías obtener**
6. **Qué no conviene hacer todavía**
7. **Cuánto puede tardar**
8. **Problemas frecuentes**
9. **Dónde verificarlo**
10. **Qué viene después**

Not every page needs all ten headings literally; preserve the information functions.

## 6. Source cards

At the bottom or near relevant callouts provide human-readable sources:

```text
Fuente oficial
Licenciatura en Ciencias de Datos — Tutorías
Verificada: 11 ago 2026
[Ver fuente ↗]
```

Do not force users to decode claim IDs.

## 7. Thesis content

The thesis is a sub-journey, not one checkbox.

Cover:

- readiness to begin;
- topic/director;
- work plan;
- thesis-course milestones;
- manuscript;
- jury review;
- corrections;
- defense;
- grade/academic closure;
- director/co-director eligibility where useful.

Do not oversimplify the thesis into "aprobar tesis".

## 8. Screenshots and examples

Screenshots can be valuable for:

- locating a TAD procedure;
- identifying an upload field;
- recognizing a correct payment receipt;
- understanding SIET chronology.

However:

- do not publish screenshots containing PII;
- sanitize names, document numbers, expediente numbers, email, identifiers;
- prefer current screenshots;
- screenshots are explanatory examples, not normative evidence;
- if an official UI changes, the text guidance must remain usable.

## 9. Link discoverability

A page may collect otherwise scattered official links. That is a core product feature.

Keep outbound actions close to the relevant next step rather than hiding them in a
generic "Links" page.

## 10. Editorial changes after freeze

If a page exposes a contradiction in the v1.0 registry:

- do not silently rewrite the claim in prose;
- record the contradiction;
- update the contract explicitly in a new version;
- then update dependent content/tests.
